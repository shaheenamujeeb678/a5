Institutional Privacy Policy

At Minute Repeater, accessible via minuterepeater.com, safeguarding the confidentiality and integrity of all client records is our highest corporate priority. We maintain strict administrative safeguards to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All electronic communications, bespoke appointments, and transactional records are encrypted using modern industrial security standards. We do not sell or lease your proprietary identity to outside advertising syndicates under any circumstance whatsoever at any time.

When utilizing our services or communicating with our team, we collect necessary contact information including your full name, email address, and direct telephone number. This information allows our staff to coordinate consultations, fulfill requested commissions, and maintain transparent service documentation. Furthermore, our web servers automatically log standard network telemetry including internet protocol addresses, browser types, and timestamped page visits to ensure optimal platform performance and security across all user browsing sessions.

Our digital infrastructure employs strictly essential session cookies to facilitate rapid navigation, remember user accessibility preferences, and ensure seamless form submission. These technical cookies do not harvest external tracking records or monitor your personal browsing habits beyond our primary domain. Users may adjust their private browser preferences to disable non-essential cookie storage at any time without compromising essential navigation functions on this website or accessing historical archives.

We store client records exclusively for durations necessary to fulfill explicit business purposes, complete active commissions, and satisfy mandatory legal documentation rules. Once our service relationship concludes and statutory storage periods lapse, your identifiable records are permanently erased from our secure cloud infrastructure. You maintain full statutory rights to request an audited extract of all personal data held in our systems at any moment in time.

We reserve the unilateral operational authority to update this institutional policy whenever statutory frameworks change or our internal technical systems undergo modernization. Continued engagement with our digital services following published revisions constitutes formal acceptance of our modernized administrative protocols, ethical standards, and client protection measures across our entire atelier.
